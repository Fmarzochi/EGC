# Responsible use

The Content Scrubber removes machine-readable provenance marks and hygiene
problems from content you own or are authorized to process.

## Appropriate

- Privacy: strip invisible carriers and metadata from your own files before
  sharing.
- Engineering hygiene: remove invisible Unicode that breaks diffs, search, or
  paste, and normalize dashes to the project style.
- Research: understand how these marks work.

## Not appropriate

- Academic fraud or misrepresenting AI assistance where disclosure is required.
- Circumventing lawful transparency or platform disclosure rules.
- Claiming cleaned content is "human-written" for compliance theater.

A removed mark does not mean the content was never AI-assisted. Use this
honestly.

## Honesty in reports

Always separate:

1. **Verifiable** removals: invisible Unicode and dashes, with counts.
2. **Best-effort** statistical rewrite: no guarantee, no "undetectable" claim.
3. **Out of scope**: pixel/audio/video watermarks, C2PA soft binding, and model
   backdoors.

Do not imply that a clean means "no AI provenance left." Point users at vendor
verification tools when they need a residual check.
